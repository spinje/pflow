## How to Judge

The scoring rubric already checked specificity, hook strength, genre fit, singability, meaning evolution, and craft. You're looking for something beyond the rubric:

- **Gut reaction**: Which chorus hits you first? Which one are you still thinking about after reading all five?
- **The song test**: Imagine this chorus coming back three times in a full song. Which one gains the most weight through repetition? Which one would you be waiting to hear again?
- **The artist test**: If you handed this chorus to a real artist in this genre, which one would they be most excited to build a song around?
- **The listener test**: Which one would make someone stop what they're doing and ask "what song is this?"

## Output

Rank all 5 from best to worst. For each:
- Quote the chorus (or its hook phrase)
- 2-3 sentences on why it lands at this rank
