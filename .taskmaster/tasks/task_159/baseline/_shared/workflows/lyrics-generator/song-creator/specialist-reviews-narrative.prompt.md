## What You're Checking

### Arc Fidelity
Does each section accomplish its INTENDED narrative job from the blueprint? Don't evaluate in the abstract — compare the actual lyrics section-by-section against what was planned.

### The Question Chain
Does each section leave the listener leaning into a question they need answered? Every section should either answer a question the previous section raised (satisfaction) or raise a new one (momentum) — ideally both. If a section neither answers nor raises a question, the listener's forward momentum stalls and they drift. Trace the chain: what question does V1 raise? Does the chorus address it? What new question does V2 raise?

### The Verse Progression Test
Does verse 2 ADVANCE the story beyond verse 1? This is the single most common failure in AI-written songs: verses that restate the same idea in different words. Verse 2 must add new information, shift perspective, escalate, complicate, or reveal something. If you could swap verse 1 and verse 2 without the listener noticing, the arc has failed.

Do verses get MORE SPECIFIC as the song progresses? The truth should get closer, not further away. If the sharpest, most concrete verse is the first one, the song's narrative trajectory is working against its emotional trajectory. Vague → specific is a story. Specific → vague is a retreat.

### The Chorus Shift Test
The chorus words stay the same each time. Does their MEANING change based on the verse that precedes them? The first chorus should land one way. After verse 2, it should hit differently. If the chorus feels identical every time it appears, the verses aren't doing their job of recontextualizing it.

### The Bridge Test
Does the bridge TURN something? A bridge must earn its existence by offering: a revelation, a perspective shift, an admission, a reversal, or a moment of clarity that changes the meaning of everything before it. A bridge that simply reduces energy before the final chorus is wasted space.

### The Redundancy Check
Does ANY section simply restate what another section already said, just with different words? Every section should add something the song doesn't have without it.

### The Arrival Test
Does the song ARRIVE somewhere? The listener should feel different at the last line than at the first line. If the emotional state at the end is the same as the beginning, the song went nowhere.
