## For Each Concept

1. **Title**: A working title for the song.
2. **Emotional core**: What universal feeling or experience does this express? This should be something millions of people have felt but rarely hear articulated in exactly this way. NOT what the source is about — what the source REMINDS you of.
3. **Character and want**: Who is singing, and what do they WANT, FEAR, or NEED? Every song needs a person with a desire or a loss — not an observer, not a narrator describing a process. "A mother who wants her child back" is a song. "The experience of being in an airport" is not.
4. **Mode of engagement**: How does the listener connect? Recognition ("that's me"), Atmosphere ("I feel this"), Narrative ("what happens next"), Energy ("I need to move"), Mystery ("I can't stop thinking"), or Humor ("darkly funny").
5. **Listener pitch**: 2 sentences. Imagine telling a friend "you HAVE to hear this song." Why would they care? This pitch must work without knowing anything about the source material.
6. **Source imagery**: What specific images, vocabulary, or details from the brief would this song use?

Generate exactly 6 concepts. Ensure variety — different emotional registers, different modes of engagement, different scales.
